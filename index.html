// 04 — Security

Security
isn't a
checkbox.

Most agencies ship a site and never run a single scan. We run the same baseline tools security teams use, and we hand you the report.

We don't provide penetration testing or compliance certification. What we do is the standard hygiene that 90% of growing-business sites are missing.

  • ▸
    HTTPS & modern TLS
    HSTS enabled. Zero mixed content.
  • ▸
    Hardened security headers
    CSP, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy.
  • ▸
    OWASP Top 10 (2021)
    Baseline scan with findings remediated or disclosed.
  • ▸
    Dependency auditing
    Every package checked. No abandoned plugins.
  • ▸
    Spam-resistant forms
    Honeypot field + Cloudflare Turnstile (free CAPTCHA alternative).
  • ▸
    Written security report
    Handed to you at launch. Scan output, what was fixed, what's monitored.

// 05 — Questions

Things we
hear a lot.

Can you really build a site in 7 days? +

Yes — for a focused 5–10 page growing-business site. We work from a tight starter template and use AI-assisted tooling for content and components. Bigger projects (e-commerce, custom apps) take longer; we'll give you a fixed date upfront.

What does "secure" actually mean here? +

It means a concrete, repeatable checklist: HTTPS, modern security headers, an OWASP Top 10 baseline scan with findings fixed or disclosed, dependency auditing, spam-resistant forms, and a written report at handover. We don't claim "unhackable" — nothing is. We do claim the basics, done properly.

What's not included? +

Ongoing content writing, social-media management, paid ads, custom application development, more than two rounds of revisions, and stock-photo licences not paid for by us. Anything outside scope is a separate quote — never a surprise charge.

Who owns the site? +

You do. The domain is in your name. Hosting accounts are in your name. The codebase is yours. We retain rights only to our reusable starter components.

How do payments work? +

50% deposit at kickoff, 50% at launch. UPI, Razorpay, or bank transfer for India. Stripe / Wise for international. Numbered invoice for every payment; GST added once our registration is active.
